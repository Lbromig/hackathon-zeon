# What is deliberately not in this directory

This repository is **public**. Three categories of material from the working preprint
folder were held back rather than pushed here.

## Photographs of people

Six camera frames were captured on the bench and are cited by the paper as its only
hardware-observed evidence. Three of them show a colleague at the workstation with
their face visible.

They are not here because publishing identifiable photographs of people to a public
repository is not a decision one contributor should make on everyone's behalf, and
because git retains them permanently even if a later commit deletes them.

They remain in the shared Drive folder. If they are wanted here, the options in
increasing order of effort are: obtain the consent of everyone visible; crop to the
deck and instrument only, which costs nothing scientifically since the deck is the
subject; or keep them in the Drive folder and cite them as unpublished.

`figures/PROVENANCE.md` in the Drive folder records what each frame can and cannot
support, and is worth reading before any of them is used as a figure. Notably `fig2`
shows that depth was acquired but supports no accuracy claim, because it is a
colourised rendering of a frame containing out-of-specification values.

## The project plan

`CONTEXT-project-plan.md` is the team's internal planning document, carried in the
Drive folder as a primary source. It records per-person ownership, unresolved
blockers, and candid assessments of what was and was not working. It is a legitimate
research artefact and it is also an internal document; publishing it is a team
decision.

Finding 16 in `FINDINGS.md` is derived from it and states its own evidence, so nothing
in the paper depends on the plan being published.

## The compiled manuscript

`Vision-Handler-Working-Preprint-v0.1.docx` and its PDF are large binaries with a
different scope and an earlier evidence cutoff than `FINDINGS.md`. Binaries in git are
awkward to review and to supersede. They are in the Drive folder.

## What is here

Every text document: the evidence base, the draft, the methods, the adversarial
fact-check, the claim-to-commit map, the mitigations supplement, and the
vision-to-pipette audit. That is the material needed to review or continue the
write-up.
